# Where the shop architecture comes from

Order **O-104** and return **R-104** run through a store based on Microsoft’s published Dynamics 365 Commerce architecture. The storefront, headless commerce engine, supply chain, customer service and customer data systems come from that design. The lesson follows one invented purchase through them. Its amounts, incidents, traces, wiring and health rules belong to this example. [Dynamics 365 Commerce architecture](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/commerce/dev-itpro/commerce-architecture)

## The agent-assisted returns extension

The shop in this lesson adds a live support agent and a background review agent. The managed runtime, model, function-calling boundary and isolated Python session follow Microsoft Foundry documentation. In this design the application runs the external order lookup and saves the receipt calculation to Blob Storage. The payout worker follows a fixed procedure after approval. Reconciliation compares the payment receipt with the merchant’s ledger. ## What Sattle adds

The [illustrated master graph](case.js) names 46 entities and 68 typed relationships at the first version. The [worked projection](projection.js) assigns every entity to one of 14 business capabilities. It retains 32 internal links as detail and lifts the other 36 to 35 typed links. The display bundles these by their 30 directed pairs of capabilities. For instance, the refund worker reads the case store and requires an approval. Both records support the business-level link from **Payout** to **Returns intake**. The tree in the guide is one way to browse the resulting graph. [White paper, chapter 5.8](../../../paper/ch05-projection-language.md#58-levels-of-abstraction-a-projection-lifts-the-edges-with-the-nodes)

A link can mean “hosts”, “reads”, “sends a message to” or “requires”. Only the health rules decide which links carry a failure into a business flow. A team can maintain the ontology that gives these links meaning and select or refine the rules. The interactive retail examples calculate from their local scenario.
